Week 5 - Documentation

1. Why are the variables declared as private?
The variables are private to protect the data. They cannot be changed directly from outside the class.

2. What happens if the variables are declared public?
If the variables are public, anyone can access and change the data directly. This is not secure.

3. Why do we use getters and setters?
We use getters and setters to safely read and update private variables. This is called encapsulation.
